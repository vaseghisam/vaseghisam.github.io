A Gumbel law for Markovian super-coupon collection

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

An \(r\)-element subset of an \(n\)-element universe evolves by replacing a uniformly chosen element with a uniformly chosen element outside the current set. Each configuration collects all of its \(s\)-element subsets. For every fixed pair \(2\le s<r\), we prove that the expected time to collect all \(s\)-subsets is asymptotic to \[\frac{\binom ns}{\binom{r-1}{s-1}}\log\binom ns.\] This proves the fixed-parameter off-diagonal case of the conjecture of Athreya, Mukherjee and Mukherjee. We also obtain a Poisson limit for the number of subsets still missing near completion and a standard Gumbel limit for the centered and scaled collection time. The conclusions hold from every initial configuration. The proof controls joint avoidance of any fixed family of target subsets, uniformly over their overlaps. Restricting the walk to the labels in that family gives a finite transition matrix, and a stationary-flow identity determines its leading killing rate. Standard spectral and factorial-moment arguments then give the collection-time limits.
