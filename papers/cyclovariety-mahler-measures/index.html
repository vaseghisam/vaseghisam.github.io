A uniform logarithmic lower bound for cyclovariety Mahler measures

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Let Cₖ be the cyclovariety associated with all k-th roots of unity in the construction of Cornelissen, Hokken and Ringeling. We prove, for every integer k ≥ 2, m(Cₖ) ≥ (1/6) log k − 1/4 + 1/(3k) − 1/(12k²). Together with the known upper bound, this gives m(Cₖ) ≍ log k uniformly in k, and proves their Conjecture 9.2.1 for every odd integer n > 1. The proof combines Jensen’s formula, an elementary count of balanced quadruples of roots of unity, and the classical second-moment tail inequality. Only the second and fourth moments of the associated Laurent polynomial are needed; no independence among its monomials is assumed.
