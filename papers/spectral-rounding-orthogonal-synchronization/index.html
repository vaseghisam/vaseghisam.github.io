Research preprint · Matrices and spectral methods

A dimension-independent spectral rounding bound for orthogonal synchronization

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab

Abstract

Spectral methods for orthogonal synchronization relax the orthogonality constraints and round the blocks to the orthogonal group. We bound the objective value after this rounding in terms of the spectral relaxation. On any finite connected weighted graph with labels in O(r), let γ be the normalized scalar Laplacian gap and let Λᵣ be the sum of the lowest r normalized connection Laplacian eigenvalues. Nearest-orthogonal rounding of the degree-normalized bottom spectral frame gives normalized squared-Frobenius frustration ν(Q) ≤ (3 + 12/γ)Λᵣ/r. The coefficient is independent of r and depends on the graph only through γ. The proof controls the positive magnitudes of the spectral blocks through a classical matrix absolute-value inequality, graph Poincaré and the frame’s second-moment normalization. We compare this certificate with the dimension-dependent connection-Cheeger bound and two dimension-independent optimum-relative estimates: a Frobenius perturbation deduction with graph dependence and a comparison using the next connection eigenvalue. No closeness to a consistent assignment is assumed.
