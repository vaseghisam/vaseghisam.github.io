Local return moments and certified random-solving times for Rubik's Cube

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Consider the random walk on the standard 3 × 3 × 3 Rubik’s Cube that independently chooses one of the eighteen face turns with equal probability. Let N denote the number of legal configurations and let Tₑ be the first hitting time of the solved configuration, including time zero. We give the certified bounds 1.0728316 < EπTₑ/N < 1.0729459. The same outward-rounded interval holds for EₓTₑ/N for every configuration whose face-turn distance from solved is at least thirteen. The proof combines the established Green-function hitting-time identity with a degree-six polynomial resolvent certificate, exact return-word counts through length twelve, and a symmetry-averaged diameter estimate giving a spectral gap of at least 1/600. The return counts were previously published in OEIS A061713 and are reproduced here by two independently implemented enumerations; all final inequalities use rational arithmetic. The certificate uses the classical matrix-moment framework to obtain an explicit finite-cube bound without computing the full spectrum or simulating rare solutions.
