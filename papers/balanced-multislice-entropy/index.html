Research preprint · Probability and entropy

Exact entropy constants for balanced multislices

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Consider the uniform measure \(\mu\) on words with \(m\) colours, each appearing \(r\) times, and assign unit weight to every unordered pair of coordinates. If \(Q(f)\) is the sum of the pair conditional entropies, we prove that the largest coefficient \(\kappa\) in \(\kappa\operatorname{Ent}_\mu(f)\leq Q(f)\) is \(\binom m2r^2\log 2/\log((mr)!/(r!)^m)\). A singleton indicator attains equality. This establishes the balanced case of the multicolour formula proposed by Bristiel and Caputo and determines the optimal logarithmic edge-expansion coefficient of the associated transposition graph. The proof combines their exact Bernoulli--Laplace entropy constant with Salez's conditioning on the complete position set of one colour. Equal multiplicities make the resulting induction close without loss. We also record the resulting classical log-Sobolev bounds and give a self-contained proof of the known binary input.
