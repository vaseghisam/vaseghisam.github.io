A commutator estimate for the Golden–Thompson gap in qubit chains

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab

Abstract

For a finite qubit chain with bounded nearest-neighbor interactions, split the interaction sum into its odd and even bond layers A and B. We compare the logarithmic trace gap F(β) = log{τ(e^(βA)e^(βB))/τ(e^(β(A+B)))} with E = τ([A,B]*[A,B]), where τ is the normalized trace. If every bond has operator norm at most J, then β⁴E/48 ≤ F(β) ≤ β⁴E/16 for 0 ≤ βJ ≤ 1/16000. The constants and interval are independent of chain length and remain valid when local commutator contributions nearly cancel. The proof retains two occurrences of the full commutator in an ordered-cumulant expansion before applying local estimates. A spanning-tree bound and a matrix row-and-column estimate then control the full Taylor remainder relative to E. The result gives a one-step partition-function certificate and a converse on the stated high-temperature interval. An exactly solvable four-site example illustrates the role of cancellation.
