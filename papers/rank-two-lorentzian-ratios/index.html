Sharp constants for bounded ratios of rank-two Lorentzian matrices

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Huang, Huh, Soskin and Wang characterized bounded ratios of Lorentzian matrix entries by cut inequalities. We prove that every normalized bounded ratio has supremum at most two on matrices of rank at most two. The supremum equals two exactly when the indices admit an ordering in which every proper initial cut is tight. In particular, the seven-index ratio that exceeds two in Zhou's rank-three example has rank-two supremum exactly two. We prove the coefficientwise conjecture of Huang, Huh, Soskin and Wang for all integral hypermetric exponents and find the optimal multiplier eight for each of the two six-index hypermetrics they single out. For bounded integral ratios whose denominator uses each edge of the complete bipartite graph \(K_{2,N}\) once, we compute both the numerical supremum and the least coefficientwise multiplier. For every bounded integral ratio, the \(k\)th root of the least coefficientwise multiplier for its \(k\)th power converges to the rank-two numerical supremum of the original ratio.
