Catalyst-rank limits for second-order entanglement concentration

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Exact catalysts can improve the second-order yield of pure-state entanglement concentration, but the optimal yield does not specify how the catalyst must grow. We obtain a necessary rank condition for this improvement. Let the source have a fixed nonuniform finite squared Schmidt vector, with entropy \(H\) and information variance \(V\) defined using base-two logarithms, and fix a required squared fidelity \(q\in(0,1)\). For pure outputs of \(n\) source copies, with a pure catalyst returned exactly and uncorrelated, suppose that the target contains \(nH+b\sqrt n+o(\sqrt n)\) Bell pairs. If the catalyst rank \(K_n\) satisfies \(\limsup \log_2K_n/\sqrt n\le c<\infty\), we give an explicit necessary fidelity bound strictly below \(e^{-b^2/(2V)}\) for every \(b>0\). Consequently, attaining the known unrestricted coefficient \(\sqrt{2V\ln(1/q)}\) requires \(\log_2K_n/\sqrt n\to\infty\). Catalysts with \(\log_2K_n=o(\sqrt n)\) cannot improve the unassisted coefficient. The proof combines a fidelity concentration estimate, a Gaussian limit of the majorization inequalities, and a positive tail term retained by the rank constraint.
