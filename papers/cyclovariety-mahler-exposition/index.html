A uniform logarithmic lower bound for cyclovariety Mahler measures: Expanded mathematical exposition

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

For the cyclovarieties Cₖ of Cornelissen, Hokken and Ringeling, we prove m(Cₖ) ≥ (1/6) log k − 1/4 + 1/(3k) − 1/(12k²) for k ≥ 2. Together with the known upper bound, this gives uniform logarithmic growth and proves their Conjecture 9.2.1 for every odd integer n > 1. This exposition develops the proof from its algebraic, analytic and probabilistic ingredients. We explain how cyclotomic coordinates preserve additive relations, derive the extra-variable Jensen identity with its integrability justification, count the second and fourth moments exactly, and obtain both a uniform comparison and the explicit lower bound. The monomials may be dependent. Figures illustrate the exact constructions and estimates; no numerical experiment is used as evidence for the theorem.
