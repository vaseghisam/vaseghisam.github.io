Research preprint · Probability and entropy

Uniform stability of approximately idempotent laws on finite groups

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab

Abstract

For every C > 2, a probability law on a finite group with sufficiently small self-convolution defect is within C times that defect of the uniform law on a subgroup, in full ℓ¹ norm. The threshold depends only on C, independently of the group, and no symmetry or commutativity assumption is required. The coefficient 2 cannot hold at any universal positive threshold. The proof obtains approximate stochastic factors from the corrected small-projection theorem of Zippin, then uses translation invariance to recover an exact permutation action and a partition into subgroup cosets. A local estimate converts this coarse approximation into the linear bound. We give an explicit sufficient threshold for C = 4 and separate the nonabelian reconstruction from the abelian consequence of Saeki’s norm-gap theorem.
