Partial Gaussian splitting in quadratic Wasserstein distance

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Let \(\mu=e^{-V}\,dx\) be a smooth probability measure on \(\mathbb{R}^n\) with \(\nabla^2V\succeq I_n\). For \(1\le k\le n\), suppose that \(u_1,\ldots,u_k\) are centered, variance-one Sobolev functions whose \(k\) gradients are orthogonal in \(L^2(\mu)\), and let \[D=\sum_{i=1}^k\left(\int|\nabla u_i|^2\,d\mu-1\right).\] We prove that there is a \(k\)-dimensional subspace \(E\) for which the squared quadratic Wasserstein distance from \(\mu\) to the product of a standard Gaussian on \(E\), translated by the projected mean, and the actual marginal of \(\mu\) on \(E^\perp\) is at most \(15D\). The estimate has no ambient-dimension factor and requires no smallness assumption on \(D\). The proof combines the Caffarelli contraction and Gaussian near-affinity estimates with a simultaneous selection of \(k\) directions and an explicit coupling that preserves the complementary marginal.
