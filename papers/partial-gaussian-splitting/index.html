Partial Gaussian splitting in quadratic Wasserstein distance

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab

Abstract

Let µ = e^(−V) dx be a smooth probability measure on ℝⁿ with ∇²V ⪰ Iₙ. For 1 ≤ k ≤ n, suppose that u₁, …, uₖ are centered, variance-one Sobolev functions whose k gradients are orthogonal in L²(µ), and let D = Σᵢ(∫|∇uᵢ|² dµ − 1). We prove that there is a k-dimensional subspace E for which the squared quadratic Wasserstein distance from µ to the product of a standard Gaussian on E, translated by the projected mean, and the actual marginal of µ on E⊥ is at most 15D. The estimate has no ambient-dimension factor and requires no smallness assumption on D. The proof combines the Caffarelli contraction and Gaussian near-affinity estimates with a simultaneous selection of k directions and an explicit coupling that preserves the complementary marginal.
