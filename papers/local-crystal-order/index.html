Local crystal order from small low-frequency scattering in dimensions 8 and 24

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Cohen, Cohn and Viazovska identified the particle arrangements that suppress scattering over the widest possible frequency ball in dimensions 8 and 24. We prove a stability theorem that allows positive scattering throughout that ball. Suppose a stationary particle process of intensity one has finite local second moments and covariance spectral mass at most δ inside the open ball up to the first reciprocal shell of E₈ or the Leech lattice. With probability tending to one, every particle in an observation ball of radius comparable to log(1/δ)/log log(1/δ) can be matched to a distinct site of one isometric copy of the corresponding lattice inside the same ball. Each displacement is O_a(δ^a) for every fixed 0 < a < 1/12, and the fraction of vacant sites tends to zero. No minimum particle separation is assumed. The proof converts spectral mass into a bound on close pairs and applies the packing stability theorem of Böröczky, Radchenko and Ramos. For periodic configurations the hypothesis is a sum of squared density amplitudes of the kind minimized in collective-coordinate simulations.
