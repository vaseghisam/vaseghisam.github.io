Research preprint · Probability and entropy

A dimension-independent box bound from dual total correlation

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Let S be a finite nonempty set in a product of d coordinate spaces, and let Uₛ be its uniform law. We prove that some nonempty Cartesian box B satisfies |S △ B| ≤ 10 DTC(Uₛ)|S|, where dual total correlation is computed using natural logarithms. The constant does not depend on the dimension or on the coordinate alphabets. The proof follows actual coordinate slices: their average disagreement is bounded linearly by mutual information, while an exact entropy identity retains the deficit available within each slice. Selecting one slice with both costs controlled makes the induction close with the same constant. The resulting Loomis–Whitney stability estimate has coefficient 20(d − 1) under the usual normalized near-equality assumption. A two-point example shows that linear dependence on d is necessary in that normalization. The constants are not claimed sharp.
