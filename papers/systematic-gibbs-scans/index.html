Research preprint · Probability and entropy

A local-degree entropy bound for systematic Gibbs scans

Sam Vaseghi · Wisehouse Science Communication Lab, Wisehouse AB

Abstract

Let P₁, …, Pₙ be the single-site heat-bath operators of a finite probability law. Suppose that Entµ(g²) ≤ L Σᵢ ‖(I − Pᵢ)g‖²₂ and that each update fails to commute with at most Δ others. We prove that every ordered sweep contracts entropy by a factor at most LΔ²/(1 + LΔ²). Approximate tensorization with constant C and a lower bound b on positive full one-site conditional probabilities gives L = C[2 + 2 log(1/b)]. The estimate permits constrained support and prescribed orders that vary between sweeps. Only full one-site conditional probabilities are bounded below; no lower bound under arbitrary partial conditioning is required. The proof follows square roots of the evolving densities, bounds their terminal local residuals using commutation, and charges their squared movement to entropy loss. Applied to the established fully frustrated hypercube Ising model with n = 2d sites and fixed inverse temperature 0 < a < 1/2, the estimate gives Oₐ(d^(5/2) log(n/ε)) complete sweeps from every starting state, uniformly over prescribed update orders. We compare this conclusion with consequences of earlier proofs and with the faster bound available for a chosen checkerboard order.
